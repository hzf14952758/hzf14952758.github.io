About

I am a computer science student at XJTLU Entrepreneur College (Taicang), currently working through a module on Networking and Distributed Computing. Most of what I enjoy sits at the boundary between the two: what happens to a packet between two processes, and what happens when you have to do the same work on many machines at once.

My approach is deliberately empirical. When I build something I try to get real numbers out of it — latency, loss, throughput — and I write down what went wrong on the way there, because the debugging is usually the part that actually teaches me something.

This page is itself one of those exercises: a static site served by GitHub Pages, which is why you can read about the network analysis of this very page in the report that accompanies it.

Projects

Video streaming over TCP and UDP

Python · sockets

A frame-by-frame video streaming client and server written directly on the socket API, in both reliable and unreliable flavours, then compared with Wireshark captures.

Headline result: dropping 2% of datagrams destroyed 55.8% of frames — and the measured value matched the (1−(1−p)n) model to within a quarter of a percentage point.
